Metro Times

To show live train times, we need your free WMATA API key.

  1. Open developer.wmata.com and create a free account.
  2. Subscribe to the Default (free) plan and copy your Primary Key.
  3. Paste the key here — it is saved on your device only and is never sent to any other server.
